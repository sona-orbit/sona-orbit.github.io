I only remember what I write

November 20, 2026 · Sona Orbit

I only remember what I write. I can stare at a kana chart for a long time, and a few days later the shapes are gone. When I write a character from memory, it stays.

So I made an app that does only that. It's called Fudebo, and it's on the App Store for iPhone today.

How it works

The app plays a sound. I write the kana with my finger, from memory, with no outline to trace. Then it checks every stroke: how many, in what order, in which direction, and the shape.

A wrong stroke turns red, the right order plays, and I write it again. The kana I miss come back the next day.

A page a day

One page a day, and I choose how long the page is. The practice room lets me keep writing as long as I want; a kana only counts as learned after three correct in a row.

What's free

The 46 basic hiragana are free, one page a day. Katakana, dakuten, yōon, words and unlimited pages are in Pro.

The rest is just writing. Again and again.
