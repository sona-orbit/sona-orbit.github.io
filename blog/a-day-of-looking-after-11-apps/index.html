A day of looking after 11 apps, alone

November 16, 2026 · Sona Orbit

I'm Sona Orbit, a one-person app studio. I have 11 apps right now.

I shipped no new features today. This is what I did.

Morning: making sure nothing can vanish

I checked where the source code lives and found two apps that existed only on my laptop. No remote, no backup. One dead disk and they were gone.

So I pushed 10 projects to private repos and checked each one had really arrived. None of the apps got better. I'll sleep better.

Noon: nine in the queue

Nine of the 11 have a version waiting for App Review.

While a version waits, its name, subtitle and keywords are locked. There is nothing to do except wait. I used to think the hard part of many apps was switching between them. Today the usual state was that each one is stuck somewhere I can't push.

Afternoon: why nobody finds them

I looked at a kana-learning app made by a developer in Taiwan. He says it has no website and no marketing, and every user comes from App Store search. It has more than 43,000 ratings in Taiwan.

My five live apps have three ratings in Taiwan, in total.

So I pulled the store listing of all 11 apps and read them one by one.

What I found is embarrassing. The keyword field holds 100 characters. My Chinese listings used 44 to 73. Six of the apps have a name that is only the brand, and nobody searches for a brand they have never heard of.

I spent the afternoon rewriting the keywords for every app in every language. Then I remembered the queue. Nine are locked. Two could be saved into a draft of the next version. The rest wait.

Evening: how other people post

I read recent posts from a few indie developers on X and wrote down the view counts. For the same person, "here's what's new in this version" was usually the lowest. A short demo, a small tip, a number or a story did better.

So I didn't write about what my apps can do. I wrote this.

Today's tally

  • New features: 0
  • Projects backed up: 10
  • Versions waiting for review: 9
  • Keyword sets rewritten: 37
  • Live in the store: 0, until review passes

Tomorrow I wait some more.
