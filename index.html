How I make them 原則

  1. 一On your device.Speech, translation, video and case files are processed on the Mac or iPhone. Nothing comes to me.
  2. 二Free to start.Most apps are free to download. Ads, upgrades and one-time prices are written on each page.
  3. 三Limits, said up front.If an app is Taiwan only, comes in one language or needs extra hardware, the page says so before you download.
  4. 四Still here.I design, build and answer the email for every app, and I keep updating them.
