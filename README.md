# Sona Orbit — small indie apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Sona Orbit is an independent studio of one person (and one dog) making small apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Design, code, the websites and the support inbox are all done by the same person, with care for where your data goes. This repository is the source of the Sona Orbit home page.

[Website](https://sona-orbit.github.io/) · [All apps](https://sona-orbit.github.io/apps/) · [Work diary](https://sona-orbit.github.io/blog/) · Contact: sona.orbit.hi@gmail.com

## Apps

- [Parroting (學舌鳥)](https://sona-orbit.github.io/parroting/) — Mac and iPhone. Hold a key, speak, and type into any app.
- [Konjac Translate (翻譯蒟蒻)](https://sona-orbit.github.io/konjac/) — iPhone. Offline translation for travel and work: face-to-face, phrase cards and a translation keyboard.
- [Yoga Glow (瑜光 30)](https://sona-orbit.github.io/yoga-glow/) — iPhone. Folds a whole yoga class video into about 30 seconds of time-lapse.
- [Stashlight (深藏)](https://sona-orbit.github.io/deepstash/) — iPhone. Home inventory: one photo saves an item, with where it is, how many are left and when it expires.
- [Trial Toolbox (審判工具箱)](https://sona-orbit.github.io/trial-toolbox/) — Mac, iPhone and iPad. Thirteen case-file tools for lawyers and paralegals in Taiwan.
- [Shoo! Stickers](https://sona-orbit.github.io/shoo/) — Mac. A hand-drawn sticker book for your Mac desktop that hides the windows you pick.
- [Boyfriend Cam (男朋友相機)](https://sona-orbit.github.io/boyfriend-cam/) — iPhone. A camera that coaches you through her photo, one composition move at a time.
- [Guomu (過目)](https://sona-orbit.github.io/guomu/) — iPhone. Opens the files your iPhone can't: Markdown, HTML, JSON, CSV, ZIP, subtitles, fonts and more.
- [Lion on the Line (有獅找你)](https://sona-orbit.github.io/lion-call/) — iPhone and iPad. Animal-village characters phone your child and ask for help with eating, tidying up or brushing teeth.
- [Fudebo](https://sona-orbit.github.io/fudebo/) — iPhone. Japanese kana handwriting from memory, checked stroke by stroke.
- [Chengshi (乘時)](https://sona-orbit.github.io/chengshi/) — iPhone. Personal timing with the traditional Chinese calendar.
- [Ringside (圈拍)](https://sona-orbit.github.io/ringside/) — iPhone. Taiwan's court and enforcement-agency property auctions on one map.
- [Fudo (不動修行)](https://sona-orbit.github.io/fudo/) — iPhone. A daily steadiness practice, a few minutes at a time.
- [Pandays (盼日)](https://sona-orbit.github.io/pandays/) — iPhone and iPad. Countdown and anniversary widgets with your own character.
- [Momo Dog (摸摸狗)](https://sona-orbit.github.io/momo-dog/) — iPhone. Stroke a sleeping dog with your finger; the fur bends, springs back, and the dog falls fast asleep.
- [Ear for Omi](https://sona-orbit.github.io/ear-for-omi/) — iPhone. A third-party iPhone app for the Omi pendant.

## About this repository

- `index.html` is the home page (projects, Threads and email contact). `*-icon-192.webp` are small product icons (192×192 WebP) shown at 56px on the home page, kept here so the home page does not depend on each product site's file names; `parroting-icon.png` stays as the favicon.
- `googleb3640a59759f7405.html` is the Google Search Console verification file. Do not delete it.
- `robots.txt` / `sitemap.xml` are search engine hints. The sitemap also lists the Parroting pages (served from the `sona-orbit/parroting` repo under `/parroting/`); update it when Parroting adds or removes public pages.
- All site content and commit messages are in English. Published by Sona Orbit. Issues and pull requests are turned off.
